Special Bimire Village Package

From Kathmandu to the hills of Bimire

A flexible village-and-trekking experience linking local trails, family homestays, Sailung Dada, and an optional mountain flight from Manthali Airport.

01 / ARRIVE

Kathmandu → Manthali

Travel from Kathmandu to Manthali and settle in before continuing into the countryside. The route and transport can be arranged to suit your group.

02 / WALK & STAY

Manthali → Chisapani → Bimire

Continue towards Chisapani and hike to Bimire through mountain scenery and local settlements. Enjoy a welcoming homestay and time with the community.

03 / TREK

Bimire → Sailung Dada

Trek with a local guide through beautiful hills, seasonal rhododendron, village landscapes, and everyday rural life. Stay in a local homestay along the way.

04 / RETURN

Sailung Dada → Bimire

Return to Bimire, take time to enjoy the village, and share another evening with local hosts before heading back.

05 / RIVERSIDE

Bimire → Manthali

Travel back to Manthali, near the Tamakoshi River, with time to appreciate the river valley and the changing mountain landscape.

06 / OPTIONAL FLIGHT

Manthali Airport → Everest views

If operating schedules and weather allow, add an Everest mountain flight from Manthali Airport. This is an optional extra, not included in the ground package.

The itinerary can be adjusted to your walking pace, season, group size, and local conditions. Mountain flights depend on airline schedules, weather, and availability.

Discover more of Nepal

Programs & Experiences

From living festivals and heritage towns to Himalayan trekking, tea gardens, and community encounters, explore Nepal in a way that suits your interests. Every program can be customized by season, time, group size, and budget.

CULTURE & FESTIVALS

Dashain, Tihar & Living Traditions

Experience Nepal's major festivals, family traditions, local celebrations, food, music, and community life. Dates and activities depend on the festival calendar.

CULTURAL HERITAGE

Janakpur Tour

Discover Janaki Temple, Mithila art, local culture, and the stories and traditions of the Terai region.

HISTORY & HERITAGE

Kathmandu Valley Tour

Explore historic squares, temples, stupas, traditional towns, architecture, and the rich cultural heritage of the valley.

NATURE & CULTURE

Pokhara, Lumbini & Chitwan

Combine lakes and Himalayan scenery in Pokhara, the birthplace of Buddha in Lumbini, and wildlife and nature experiences in Chitwan.

TEA & COMMUNITY

Eastern Nepal Tea Gardens & Women's Community

Visit tea-growing landscapes in eastern Nepal and learn about tea production, local livelihoods, and the experiences and contributions of women in the community.

HIMALAYAN TREKKING

Annapurna Base Camp (ABC)

Trek through mountain villages, forests, and dramatic Himalayan landscapes towards Annapurna Base Camp, with planning based on fitness, season, and conditions.

HIMALAYAN TREKKING

Everest Base Camp (EBC)

A classic high-altitude adventure through Sherpa villages and spectacular mountain scenery, planned with appropriate acclimatization and preparation.

DAY HIKE

Changunarayan to Nagarkot

Walk from the historic Changunarayan area towards Nagarkot through village paths, countryside, and scenic hill landscapes.

HERITAGE HIKE

Dhulikhel → Namobuddha → Panauti

Enjoy a rewarding walk linking hill views, the Buddhist pilgrimage site of Namobuddha, and the historic Newari town of Panauti.

CULTURE & COMMUNITY

Kirtipur, Tribhuvan University & Pharping

Explore Kirtipur's Newari heritage, the university area, and the spiritual sites and peaceful surroundings of Pharping.

CUSTOM JOURNEYS

Many More Programs

Ask us about village homestays, local food and crafts, volunteering and internship experiences, community visits, customized family trips, and other destinations across Nepal.

Trekking routes, permits, transport, accommodation, guide services, and inclusions are confirmed individually for each itinerary. High-altitude treks require suitable preparation and acclimatization.
